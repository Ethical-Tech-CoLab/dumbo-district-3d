DUMBO tree fixtures

Every tree prototype the build produced, across four seasons and the three maturities the Forestry census actually contains. Each trio is young · establishing · mature, drawn to the proportions the viewer uses. 29 prototypes, 1306 trees placed.

Position, species and trunk diameter are grade A from the NYC Forestry census (DSRC-009). Canopy form and seasonal colour are plausible for the genus rather than measured, so the rendered prop stays graded C. Summer foliage is additionally pulled towards the colour measured from photographs of DUMBO. Back to the viewer
